The thing about scale

Many startups fail because they grew faster than their understanding of who they were for. I wrote about this last month and got a lot of pushback — most of it constructive feedback.

The pattern: founder solves their own problem. Solves it well. Then VCs ask "but for whom else?" and the team tries to retrofit a market.

What stayed small and worked

37signals. Pinboard. Are.na. Each of these stayed deliberately under-scaled and made more enduring products for it. More on this in part two next week.
